Building cleaner, healthier and more sustainable cities

Introduction

Waste is a major challenge in growing cities. Proper waste management helps keep our surroundings clean and protects the environment.

A zero waste city focuses on reducing waste, reusing useful materials, recycling and managing waste responsibly.

Waste Management

Solid waste management includes collecting, separating, transporting, treating and disposing of waste safely.

Good waste management reduces pollution and helps cities become cleaner and healthier.

The 3Rs

Reduce

Avoid buying or using things that create unnecessary waste.

Reuse

Use products again instead of throwing them away.

Recycle

Recycle materials such as paper, plastic, glass and metal whenever possible.

Waste Segregation

Waste segregation means separating waste into different categories before disposal.

Wet Waste

Food scraps, vegetable peels and other biodegradable waste.

Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, plastic, glass and other recyclable materials.

E-Waste

Old electronic items such as phones, computers and other electronic equipment.

Simple Solutions

✓ Carry reusable bags and bottles.

✓ Avoid unnecessary single-use products.

✓ Separate wet and dry waste.

✓ Reuse useful materials.

✓ Recycle recyclable waste.

✓ Compost suitable organic waste.
